Note: Atemkraft is available in German only. All texts, guidance and voices in the app are German.

Atemkraft guides you through breathing exercises and silent meditation. A circle breathes with you: it grows as you inhale and shrinks as you exhale, with optional tones and vibration.

- 17 guided breathing exercises in four areas (calming, activating, balancing, breath training), such as resonance breathing, 4-7-8 and box breathing. Each one comes with instructions, safety notes and an honest summary of the research, with references (usually DOI or PMID).
- Pick a situation such as falling asleep or focus, or search for how you feel.
- A new breathing pattern every day, and an editor to build your own.
- Meditation timer with gong and optional spoken guidance; keeps running with the screen off.
- Background reading on breathing in yoga, pranayama, a glossary and three programmes (SKY, Buteyko, nasal breathing).
- Log of completed sessions; export and import your patterns.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device; the app only goes online if you download a voice (from GitHub).
